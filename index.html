About us

GOOD CUTS.
GREAT CONFIDENCE.

Fabulous Barbershop is a unisex salon on Ebony Road in Rumuchita, Port Harcourt — inside Habee Concept. We're built around one idea: a haircut should feel personal, not rushed.

Come in for a fade, a beard shape or a full grooming session. Ask about the rest too — alongside barbing, our team handles pedicures, facials and massage, so you can leave looking and feeling brand new.

Find us at 19/20 Ebony Rd, off Oroazi Road. We're open late, until 9:30 pm.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

The things clients ask us most. Still unsure? Give the shop a call on 0802 927 2300.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but a quick call ahead saves you waiting — especially on weekends and evenings.

Where exactly is the shop?

19/20 Ebony Rd, off Oroazi Rd, Rumuchita, Port Harcourt 500272, inside Habee Concept. Plus code RXMR+37 for your maps app. Get directions ↗

What does a haircut cost?

Rates depend on the service. Call 0802 927 2300 for current prices before you come in — no surprises at the counter.

Do you cut women's hair?

Yes. Fabulous is a unisex salon — haircuts for men and women, plus pedicure, facials and massage.

What are your opening hours?

We close at 9:30 pm. Opening times can vary, so a quick call ahead is the safest way to plan your visit. Hours may change on public holidays.

Can I book for a wedding or event?

Yes — grooms, groomsmen and groups. Book a day or two ahead so we can give your party the time it needs.
